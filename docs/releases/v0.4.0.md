## OpenCheck 0.4.0

0.4.0 gathers the 0.3 releases into one version. If you come from 0.3.0, this is what is new; the notes of each 0.3 release have the details.

**Working with Claude**
- Claude Desktop sorts findings into C1–C3, makes issues when you allow it, and reads the project's own triage rules and your corrections first.
- Claude sees findings in pictures: by default only the clash's elements, close up, in their models' colours; also wider, with the rest faint, or from below. The pictures are saved for reports, and findings have IFC coordinates.

**Sorting clashes**
- Every model keeps its own colour, also for its elements in clashes and its discipline in the results; click the model's dot to change it.
- Ctrl/Shift+click clash markers to pick several clashes and accept, reject or bundle them at once, while the ticked group stays in view. Making an issue no longer moves the view.
- The rest of the model while reviewing: shown, ghosted or hidden. Markers for the ticked findings only.

**Issues**
- *Issued by* on every issue (your initials, or Claude), and a filter by who made the issues and when.

**Information requirements and reports**
- The LOD overview per IFC file with fill rates per building part, and items accepted as missing.
- Reports per phase with revisions, a section on the information level, free text sections with pictures, and a watermark for trial prints.

**Windows**
- OpenCheck has its own icon.

## Download
Download `OpenCheck-v0.4.0-windows-x64.zip` below, unzip it and run `opencheck-app.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: click *More info* and then *Run anyway*. If you use the Claude connection, restart Claude Desktop after updating.
