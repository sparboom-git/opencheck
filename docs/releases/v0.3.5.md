## What's new in 0.3.5

**Information requirements (LOD)**
- The overview is split per IFC file: a heading per file with its fill rate and, under it, the building parts checked in that file.
- A part's fill rate is the share of required values that are there, over all its items and elements, so a part with four of five items filled in reads as 80 % instead of "0 elements complete". Hover for the numbers.
- *Accept that the models lack the item* in *Where to look…*: information agreed not to be in the models is no longer reported, and shows as *OK* in blue so it stays visible that it was accepted rather than filled in.
- The *Where to look…* window no longer locks to full width after *List elements*.
- Items read from the geometry (levels and positions) are marked with a small triangle in the report, with a line saying so.

**Reports**
- New block *Afsnit: Informationsniveau*, placed after *Egenskaber* in new reports: how the check was done, in Danish, and the fill rates per IFC file and building part with every item, in the PDF, in Word and in the preview.
- Phases: a report is made for a phase (Disp, PF, MP, UBP, UFP, SU) and starts without a revision; the first issue makes it revision A. *Update data → new version* makes the next revision of the phase or starts a new phase, which begins without a revision again. The revision table shows the phase.
- The date printed in the report is typed by hand in the introduction block; empty, it is the date of issue.
- *Foreløbigt tryk* stamps a watermark across every page of the preview, the PDF and the Word document for a trial print.
- *Tekst (frit afsnit)* in *Add block* for a blank section, and screenshots in any block: *Paste picture* or Ctrl+V while editing, or a PNG file. Pictures are set to the width of the text column, a small crop at its own size, a tall one kept to half a page.

**Command line**
- `opencheck check` prints the fill rate per building part and marks items accepted as missing.
