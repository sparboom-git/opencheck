## What's new in 0.3.4

**Clear pictures for Claude**
- Pictures Claude takes of findings now show only the clash's elements, in their models' colours, with the clash filling the picture, 1200 px wide: clear pictures for a report Claude writes.
- Claude can ask for more: the rest of the model faint (`ghost`) or shown, a normal or wide framing to see where the clash is, and a view from below, e.g. into a ceiling void, besides the angle, the plan and the four elevations.
- A picture shows only its own finding, also when other findings are ticked; your ticks and view are put back afterwards.

**Fixes**
- Exclusions edited on the Check tab (*Leave out*) are kept when the project is saved.
- *Let Claude make issues* is off until you switch it on, and both Claude permissions are remembered.
- Issue viewpoints keep a parallel view.
- Level checks name their storeys, also in autorun; the default tolerance is 10 mm as documented.
- Excel templates: merged cells and validations on a repeated row stay on its first copy. Word reports drop characters Word cannot read.
- An issued report revision keeps its introduction and name.

## Download
Download `OpenCheck-v0.3.4-windows-x64.zip` below, unzip it and run `opencheck-app.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: click *More info* and then *Run anyway*. After updating, restart Claude Desktop so it picks up the new picture options.
