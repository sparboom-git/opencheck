## What's new in 0.3.0

**Reports**
- A new *Report* tab writes the documentation of a consistency and clash check: introduction, the models and their versions, the software and rulesets, properties, geometry, clashes, visual checks and a conclusion. Blocks can be added, moved, given their own text and filters, and start on a new page.
- Revisions freeze their data. *Issue revision…* locks one; *Update data → new revision* takes the latest check into the next.
- The preview shows the report in A4 pages, exactly as it prints, or as a quick draft. A click on a page edits the block there.
- *Export PDF…* and *Export Word…* write the revision shown.
- Reports are made from a Word template. *File → Settings* saves the standard one to change in Word (logo, header and footer, margins, fonts, colours, the first page) and choose again. Fields such as `{{project}}` and `{{revision}}` are filled in.

**Visual checks**
- *Document check…* writes a visual check down as done: what it checks, how it was done, the result as a traffic light (approved, approved with remarks, not approved) and pictures of the view. Reports take the documented checks in.
- *Rooms*: rooms green; places enclosed by walls without a room are found, their walls red. A button per storey shows it in plan, with each room's number and name and a label of the plan.
- *Levels*: the storeys of all models side by side by height, as a table and as levels in the 3D view. With a master model, the storeys that differ from it are marked.

**Checking**
- *Leave out…* on a ruleset card: a table of what the check leaves out. Each row is a discipline's models, a category and a property with *contains*, *is*, *is not*, *starts with*, *is under*, *is over* or *exists*, and shows how many elements it leaves out. The same table is under each rule in the Ruleset Manager.
- The discipline of a model follows the K code in its file name (K01 architecture, K07 electrical, K08 ventilation, K09 structure, …).

**Viewer**
- Parallel view (P) without perspective, and fixed views from the top and from each side, as elevations.
- The section handle follows the dark theme.
