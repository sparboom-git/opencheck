## What's new in 0.4.1

**Check**
- The rulesets list starts empty. Add the rulesets you want to run with *+ Add* at the top of the panel; they are listed as you add them and saved with the project.
- The rulesets panel can hide itself: click the pin next to *Add*, and the panel leaves a bar at the left edge and comes out over the 3D view while the mouse is on it. Click the pin again to keep it shown. The choice is remembered.
- The results panel can be dragged narrower: the filters move to the next line instead of holding the panel wide.

**Visual Check**
- The panel of checks has the same pin, with a choice of its own.
- *The other elements*: shown, ghosted or hidden, chosen per kind of check in the panel or from the X-ray button in the toolbar. The wall alignment check hides them from the start, so only the coloured walls are in view.

**Logs and crash reports**
- OpenCheck writes a log of each session and, if it crashes, a crash report next to it. If it did not close normally, the next start says so and shows where the files are, so they can be sent along with a description of what happened.
- *File → Settings → Logs and crash reports* opens the folder. The files stay on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

**WIP**
- A *WIP* switch in *File → Settings*, off until you switch it on. With it off OpenCheck offers only what is stable. With it on you also get the features still under development: the unfinished checks, and adding Revit files (`.rvt`), which are converted to IFC when they are added.

## Download
Download `OpenCheck-v0.4.1-windows-x64.zip` below, unzip it and run `opencheck-app.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: click *More info* and then *Run anyway*.
