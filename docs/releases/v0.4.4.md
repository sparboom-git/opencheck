## What's new in 0.4.4

**Coordination files (.smc, WIP)**
- With *WIP* on in *File → Settings*, a coordination file, the file a model checker (Solibri) saves with the IFC models of a project put together, can be added like an IFC file: *File → Add IFC, SMC or Revit models*, or drop it on the window. It is under development because it has been tried on few files so far. Each model in it becomes a model in OpenCheck, named after the IFC file it was read from, with its elements, GlobalIds, property sets, storeys with their elevations, colours and shapes, and is stored in the project as IFC4. Desktop app only.
- A window says what was converted: the models with their element counts, and what to be careful about (elements without a storey, classes written as a proxy, elements without a shape).
- The models are what the checker kept, not the original IFC files: shapes are its triangulation, openings are cut already, types, systems and quantities are not written. Update a model from its own IFC file with ↻ on its card when you have it: name, discipline, colour and your picks carry over, since the GlobalIds are the same.
- Command line: `opencheck convert project.smc [project.opencheck]` writes the project with one IFC per model.
- The reader of these files is from [Open BIM Suite](https://github.com/OpenAEC-Foundation/open-bim-suite) by the OpenAEC Foundation, under the LGPL-3.0-or-later. It lives in the crate `opencheck-smc`, which is under that licence as a whole; the rest of OpenCheck stays MIT or Apache-2.0. The credit, licence and notice are shown in *File → Settings → WIP*, ship with every download as `open-bim-suite-LICENSE.txt` and `open-bim-suite-NOTICE.txt`, and [docs/smc-files.md](../smc-files.md) says what is converted and what is not.

**Revit files (WIP)**
- rvt-rs is taken in at 0.25.0 (from 0.20.0). Since then: extruded roofs of Revit 2024, 2026 and 2027 as solids, foundation slabs as IfcSlab, material colours in the IFC, PredefinedType as Revit's own export gives it, curtain walls, the FireRating of doors and windows, Pset_DoorCommon and Pset_WindowCommon on Revit 2015 to 2023 files, and a fix for unbounded memory on a crafted file. On OpenCheck's Revit 2026 test file the element count is the same; six footings became slabs, 251 material colours came along, and every element now reaches its storey through its level.
- rvt-rs changed how it derives GlobalIds (0.21.0 and 0.23.0): a Revit model updated from the same `.rvt` gets new GlobalIds on about a third of its elements once, so clashes in issues on those elements are marked solved and found again as new. Models converted before keep their GlobalIds until they are updated.

**Documented checks**
- The *Document check* dialog has no Save button any more: the record is in the list of documented checks from the moment the dialog opens, every change and every picture lands in it as it is made, and closing the dialog loses nothing. *Done* closes it, *Delete…* takes the record away again. Before, closing the dialog without Save threw the record and its pictures away.

**IFC reading**
- ifc-lite is taken in at 30.0.0 (from 20.4.0, nine releases). What it brings to the models OpenCheck reads: typed property values such as `IFCLABEL('x')` are parsed as their own value; an unescaped apostrophe refuses that one record instead of swallowing the records after it; relationship lists with nested values no longer shift their references; property sets of a type reached through `IfcRelDefinesByProperties` are inherited; adjoining openings are welded before they are cut from their host, thin pieces between openings stay closed, and openings that could not be cut are counted; a mapped item without `Axis2` mirrors as it should; swept solids roll their section as ISO 10303-42 says; non-finite coordinates give an error instead of a crash; the output is deterministic. On OpenCheck's test models the elements and storeys are the same, the Revit test model has 0.8 % fewer triangles, and loading is as fast.
