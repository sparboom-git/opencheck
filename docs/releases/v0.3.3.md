## What's new in 0.3.3

**Model colours**
- Every model keeps its own colour, saved in the project: its dot on the model card, its name in the model tree, its elements in clashes and its discipline in the results. A wall from the structural model is always in the structural model's colour.
- Click the dot on the model card to choose another colour. Colours close to the green of selected elements are not offered.
- Pictures for BCF, Excel and Claude use the model colours too.
- A selected element of a clash under review is shown in green.

**Sorting clashes in 3D**
- Ctrl+click (or Shift+click) clash markers to pick several clashes. They get a crosshair, with their marker still readable inside it, and *n picked in 3D* accepts, rejects or makes one issue of them all at once. The ticks in the list stay as they are, so the clashes you look at stay in view.
- A plain click on a marker opens that one clash, and a click beside the markers lets go of it; the ticked clashes stay in view.
- Making an issue no longer moves the 3D view, so the sorting can go on.
- *Only the ticked findings* in the markers' menu (right-click the crosshair button) shows markers only for the ticked clashes, at any distance.

**The rest of the model**
- While reviewing clashes, the X-ray button gives a choice for the rest of the model: shown, ghosted (faint and grey) or hidden.

## Download
Download `OpenCheck-v0.3.3-windows-x64.zip` below, unzip it and run `opencheck-app.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: click *More info* and then *Run anyway*. After updating, restart Claude Desktop if you use the Claude connection.
